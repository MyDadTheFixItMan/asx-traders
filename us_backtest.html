How to read this

  • Per year: average yearly growth. Worst drop: the largest fall from a high point, the pain you'd have sat through.
  • Weeks hitting goal: share of weeks that made at least the weekly target.
  • "Without ..." rows switch off one upgrade, to show whether it helps. Better than "Current rules" means that upgrade hurt in this period.
  • Limits: daily prices only (the live agent checks every 5 minutes), today's S&P 500 list used for the whole period (flatters results), results dates not tested. Dividends are included through adjusted prices, without franking credits.
