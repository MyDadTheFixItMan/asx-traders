# Rule changes and why

Every change to the trading rules is recorded here, newest first, with the
evidence behind it. Changes to buy and sell rules go live only if they pass
`python -m asx_agent.evaluate`, which tests them over 3 and 9 years from four
start dates each. Fixes to how trades are carried out (timing, data checks)
can't be tested on daily prices; they are made only to stop the paper ledger
recording trades that couldn't really happen, and are recorded here too.

To undo a change, restore the setting named in its entry in `config.json`.

## 2026-11-10: US Paper Trader only: 1 stock per sector, no quality filter

- **Settings (config_us.json only; the ASX rules are unchanged):** `strategy.max_per_sector: 1`,
  `strategy.use_quality_filter: false`. Previous file:
  `data/us/config_backups/config_us_2026-11-10.json`.
- **Why:** the first 3-year test on S&P 500 prices (`reports/us/backtest_2026-11-10.md`). From 4
  start dates the ASX rules made a median +31.0% (worst start +26.7%, median worst drop -20.9%)
  against +80.5% for holding the S&P 500. With both changes: median +137.2%, worst start +67.9%,
  median worst drop -15.8%. Each change also helped on its own.
- **Caution:** today's S&P 500 list is used for the whole period, which flatters these results,
  most of all without the quality filter.
- **Undo:** set `max_per_sector` to 0 and `use_quality_filter` to true in config_us.json.

## 2026-11-08 (evening): Share-issuance filter switched on

- **Setting:** `strategy.max_share_growth_pct: 20` (previous file in
  `data/config_backups/config_2026-11-08c.json`). Skips buying a company whose
  share count grew more than 20% in its latest financial year (accounts treated
  as public 90 days after the year end). Stocks with no share figures pass.
  Holdings are not sold because of it, but it blocks adding to them.
- **Why:** companies that issue many new shares (capital raisings, takeovers
  paid in shares) tend to do worse afterwards: Pontiff & Woodgate (2008,
  Journal of Finance) in the US; on the ASX, Lancaster & Bornholt found big
  non-issuers beat big heavy issuers by about 0.84% a month over 1990–2009
  ([Quantpedia summary](https://vvv.quantpedia.com/?p=520)), and Li, Naidu,
  Navissi & Ranjeeni (2018, Australian Journal of Management) found the effect
  comes from issuers, not buybacks
  ([record](https://research.monash.edu/en/publications/net-stock-issuance-anomaly-and-cash-flow-explanation-a-research-n/)).
  Moderate, peer-reviewed evidence, including on Australian shares.
- **Evaluation** (3 years, `reports/evaluations/2026-11-08 Skip share issuance over 20 pct.md`):
  median +54.9% against +40.8%, worst start +23.7% against +20.8%, median worst
  drop −12.1% against −12.5%, 187 trades against 192. Neighbours: 15% and 30%
  gave the same result; 5% and 10% gave +55.2%, worst start +29.0% but a
  slightly deeper drop (−13.1%), which is why 20% was chosen.
- **Caution:** 15%, 20% and 30% give identical results, so the gain comes from
  skipping a handful of trades in heavy issuers (often companies that took over
  another with shares, such as ALK, JHX, WGX, RMS). Yahoo has only about four
  years of share counts, so the filter has little data at the very start of
  the 3-year test.
- **Tested and not adopted the same evening:** ranking partly on nearness to
  the 52-week high (weights 0.25, 0.5, 0.75: +33.6%, +39.3%, +17.7%) and a
  minimum buy score of 4, 5 or 6 (+46.6% passed alone but 5's drop was deeper;
  6 was −4.8%).
- **Undo:** set `max_share_growth_pct` to 0.

## 2026-11-08: Lottery-stock filter switched on

- **Setting:** `strategy.max_lottery_pct: 4` (previous file in
  `data/config_backups/config_2026-11-08b.json`). Skips a stock when the average
  of its 5 biggest daily gains over the last 21 trading days is above 4%.
- **Why:** the user asked for continued research to make money. Bali, Cakici &
  Whitelaw (2011, Journal of Financial Economics) found stocks with extreme
  recent daily gains go on to underperform; Gorman et al. (2022, Critical
  Finance Review) found the same pattern and put it down to overreaction. It
  also suits a momentum strategy, which can otherwise buy stocks straight after
  a spike.
- **Evaluation** (3 years, `reports/evaluations/2026-11-08 Skip lottery stocks MAX 4 pct.md`):
  median +49.5% against +44.4%, worst start +22.2% against +16.1%, median worst
  drop −12.0% against −13.3%. Nearby settings: 3.5% passed (+47.1%, worst start
  +30.0%); 3%, 4.5% and 5% didn't beat the median by 2 points but all had a
  better worst start than the current rules.
- **Tested and not adopted the same day:** smooth momentum, volatility targeting
  (12% and 15%) and residual momentum (see README).
- **Undo:** set `max_lottery_pct` to 0.

## 2026-11-08: Rules from trading books switched on; tests use 3 years only

- **Settings** (`config.json` → `strategy`; the previous file is in
  `data/config_backups/config_2026-11-08.json`):
  - `max_below_high_pct: 25` – buy only within 25% of the 52-week high (O'Neil, Livermore)
  - `skip_bearish_candle: true` – skip a stock on a bearish engulfing day (Nison)
  - `drawdown_cut_pct: 10` – half-size new trades while 10%+ below the peak (Market Wizards)
  - `pyramid_trigger_r: 1` (with `pyramid_size` 0.5, `pyramid_max_adds` 1,
    `pyramid_cap_mult` 1.5, `pyramid_stop_to_entry` true) – once a holding is up one
    stop distance, buy half as many shares again and raise its stop to the buy price (Livermore)
- **Why:** the user asked for rules from nine trading books and chose to switch
  these on because together they did better over the last 3 years. The user also
  decided that rules are judged on the last 3 years only, so `evaluate.py` no
  longer runs the 9-year test.
- **Evidence** (`reports/evaluations/2026-11-08 Book rules combined with Livermore adds.md`):
  - 3 years, median of four start dates: +44.4% against +37.1% for the earlier
    rules; worst start +16.1% against +17.3%; median worst drop −13.3% against −15.9%.
  - 9 years (shown to the user before deciding): −8.2% against +142.6%, worst
    drop −41.1% against −29.4%. Over 9 years each rule except the candle filter
    lost ground alone, and together they did much worse.
- **Holdings bought before this change** use their average cost as the buy price
  for the adding rule.
- **Undo:** set those four settings back to 0 / false (or restore the backup).
  To restore the 9-year test, revert the 8 Nov change to `asx_agent/evaluate.py`.

## 2026-11-04: Trader moved to GitHub, checks every 15 minutes

- **Change:** the trader runs on GitHub Actions (private repository
  `MyDadTheFixItMan/asx-trade-agent`) so the PC doesn't need to be on. The
  Windows task "ASX Paper Trader" is disabled. The ledger carried over unchanged.
- **Why every 15 minutes, not 5:** the user's choice, so the runs fit inside
  GitHub's free 2,000 minutes a month for private repositories. The rules only
  use daily prices that run about 20 minutes late, so this mostly changes how
  quickly a stop is acted on.
- **Undo:** disable the workflow on GitHub, then re-enable the Windows task.

## 2026-11-03: Trade only after 10:30, on today's prices

- **Setting:** `strategy.first_trade_time: "10:30"`, plus a check that today's prices exist.
- **Why:** on 2 Nov the agent bought six stocks between 10:00 and 10:15. Yahoo's
  prices are about 20 minutes late, so those buys used Friday's closing prices.
  The real opening prices were lower for most of them: ALK opened 3.6% below,
  WAF 4.3% below and PRU 3.4% below what the ledger paid. The ledger was
  recording fills nobody could get. Waiting until 10:30 means prices from after
  the opening auction have arrived. The same check stops trading on any day
  with no prices, such as a public holiday missing from `market_holidays`.
- **Risk:** if Yahoo ever fails to show a price bar for today, the agent won't
  trade that day. The log says "No prices for today yet" when this happens,
  and the daily check looks for it.

## 2026-11-03: Daily review and change test added

- Daily review (`asx_agent/review.py`) written at 4pm each trading day to
  `reports/daily/`, splitting each day's result into market, stock picking and costs.
- Change test (`asx_agent/evaluate.py`) that every rule change must pass.
- Instructions for a weekly research and improvement session and a short check
  after each trading day are in `automation/`. They run as Claude scheduled
  tasks: "ASX daily check" (weekdays about 4:30pm) and "ASX weekly
  improvement" (Saturdays about 9am), created 3 Nov. They run only while the
  Claude app is open; a missed run starts when the app next opens.
